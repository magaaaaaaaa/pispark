## My positions

1. Every wrong answer has a reason, and naming the reason fixes more than doing 100 more
   questions.
2. Three weeks before the test is not too late. It is enough to fix the two or three
   mistakes that cost the most and to walk in with a pacing plan.
3. The built-in Desmos calculator is the most underused point-saver on the digital SAT.
4. Nobody honest promises a score. Full attention and straight feedback, yes.
5. 70% of SAT math is algebra and advanced math, so that is where prep starts.
